## RICE (10)

### RICE-001 — Rice Blast
- **Title:** Approaches to Reduce Rice Blast Disease Using Knowledge from Host Resistance and Pathogen Pathogenicity
- **Contribution:** Synthesises isolated R, qR and Avr genes in the rice–*M. oryzae* system and how they are used in practice.
- **Why useful:** Core reference for the blast "resistant variety / gene" knowledge the assistant needs.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms24054985

### RICE-002 — Rice Blast *(Pakistan)*
- **Title:** Molecular Characterization of *Magnaporthe oryzae* in Punjab, Pakistan and its In Vitro Suppression by Fungicides and Botanicals
- **Contribution:** Punjab field survey + in-vitro fungicide/botanical screening (difenoconazole, black pepper most effective).
- **Why useful:** Local pathogen data and *experimental* control options for Pakistani Punjab.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.17957/IJAB/15.1222

### RICE-003 — Bacterial Leaf Blight
- **Title:** Resistance Genes and their Interactions with Bacterial Blight/Leaf Streak Pathogens (*Xanthomonas oryzae*) in Rice — an Updated Review
- **Contribution:** R genes and TAL-effector interactions for BLB/BLS.
- **Why useful:** Diagnosis + resistance-breeding knowledge for the BLB module.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s12284-019-0358-y

### RICE-004 — Sheath Blight
- **Title:** Sheath blight of rice: a review and identification of priorities for future research
- **Contribution:** Reviews *R. solani* pathogenicity/management and sets research priorities (biocontrol, resistance, smart detection).
- **Why useful:** Balanced management overview for the sheath-blight module.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00425-019-03246-8

### RICE-005 — Sheath Blight
- **Title:** *Rhizoctonia solani* Kühn Pathophysiology: Status and Prospects of Sheath Blight Disease Management in Rice
- **Contribution:** Pathophysiology + varietal and biotechnological management.
- **Why useful:** Deeper mechanism/management detail complementing RICE-004.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2022.881116

### RICE-006 — Brown Spot
- **Title:** Brown Spot of Rice: Worldwide Disease Impact, Phenotypic and Genetic Diversity of *Bipolaris oryzae*, and Management
- **Contribution:** Recent global review of impact, pathogen diversity and IDM.
- **Why useful:** Up-to-date brown-spot biology and management baseline.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1111/ppa.14075

### RICE-007 — Brown Spot *(Pakistan)*
- **Title:** Brown Leaf Spot: An Exacerbated Embryonic Disease of Rice: A Review
- **Contribution:** IDM strategies for brown spot in a Pakistani (UAF) context.
- **Why useful:** Local management framing (cultural, nutrient, resistant-cultivar focus).
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.17582/journal.jis/2020/6.2.108.125

### RICE-008 — False Smut
- **Title:** The Genetic Mechanism of the Immune Response to the Rice False Smut Fungus *Ustilaginoidea virens*
- **Contribution:** Symptoms, resistance genes and immune/QTL mechanisms.
- **Why useful:** Foundational false-smut biology and resistance knowledge.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.3390/plants12040741

### RICE-009 — Rice Tungro *(landmark)*
- **Title:** The biology, epidemiology, and management of rice tungro disease in Asia
- **Contribution:** Definitive Asia-wide account of tungro biology/epidemiology/management.
- **Why useful:** Anchor reference for the tungro module. **Viral — no chemical cure; vector-based management.**
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1094/PDIS.2002.86.2.88 *(confirm DOI resolves)*

### RICE-010 — Rice Tungro
- **Title:** A Review of Vector-Borne Rice Viruses
- **Contribution:** Emergence, transmission and management of rice viruses incl. tungro.
- **Why useful:** Vector-management detail for the tungro module. **Viral — manage green leafhopper, not fungicide.**
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.3390/v14102258
